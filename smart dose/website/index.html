The problem

Missing a dose shouldn't be this easy.

Nearly half of people on long-term medication don't take it exactly as prescribed — not from carelessness, but because remembering, sorting, and timing a growing list of prescriptions is genuinely hard. Pillboxes ask for organization patients don't always have. Reminder apps can nag, but they can't stop a wrong or duplicate dose. And most automated dispensers on the market today are reported to jam, misload, or bury their cancellation terms — trading one kind of stress for another.

Reminders aren't enough

An alarm can't tell you whether the right pill, at the right dose, actually left the box.

Hardware that jams

Independent reviews consistently point to the same complaint across the category: dispensers that misload or fail exactly when they're needed most.

Caregivers left guessing

Adult children managing a parent's medication from a distance are stuck piecing together phone calls and guesswork.

The smartdose approach

One mechanical promise. Everything else supports it.

Physically fail-safe

Every compartment stays sealed until its scheduled moment. Only the correct one ever rises and unseals — a tactile, unmistakable cue no notification can replace.

Caregiver visibility

Real-time status, adherence history, and escalation alerts — so the people who care from a distance always know what happened, not just what was scheduled.

AI + AR intelligence

Adaptive reminders, predictive adherence insights, and scan-to-learn medication guidance — layered on top of the core device, never in place of it.

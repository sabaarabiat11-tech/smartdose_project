Features

Everything smartdose does — and why each piece earns its place.

No feature here exists to pad a spec sheet. Every one traces back to a real failure point we found researching how patients and caregivers actually experience medication management.

Sealed, tamper-resistant compartments

Access to non-scheduled medication is physically impossible, not just discouraged.

Scheduled physical dispensing

The correct compartment rises and unseals automatically — no button-mashing, no ambiguity.

Temperature & humidity monitoring

Silent-degradation risk for heat- or moisture-sensitive medication gets caught before it matters.

QR + AR medication information

Scan any compartment to see what it is, why it's prescribed, and how to take it correctly.

AI-powered adherence analytics

Behavioral pattern detection that flags a risk before it becomes a missed dose.

Full-featured mobile app

Reminders, complete history, health insights, and caregiver notifications in one place.

Multi-user, permissioned access

Patient plus one or more caregivers, each with configurable visibility and alert thresholds.

Low-supply & refill alerts

You find out before the bottle is empty, not after.

Missed-dose escalation

Irregular patterns and missed doses notify designated caregivers automatically.

Offline fail-safe operation

The device keeps dispensing on its stored schedule even without a network connection, syncing once reconnected.

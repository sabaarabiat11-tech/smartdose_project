App features

Built for two different people, in one app.

Guided onboarding

Set up a full schedule fast — add medications by name or scan the prescription label to reduce manual entry error.

Live device status

See what's loaded, what's due, and what's running low, compartment by compartment.

Reminders & notifications

Patient-facing reminders and separate caregiver escalation alerts, with configurable thresholds.

Adherence history & reports

Exportable records you can bring to a physician or care team appointment.

Caregiver mode

Multiple caregivers, each with their own login and notification preferences.

Health insights dashboard

Trend views of adherence over time, surfaced in plain language, not just raw logs.

Medication database & interactions

Warnings surfaced at the exact moment the AR scan-to-learn feature is used.

Refill management

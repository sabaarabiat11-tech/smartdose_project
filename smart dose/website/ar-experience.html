AR Experience

Scan to understand, not just to comply.

Point your phone at any smartdose compartment and see exactly what it is, why it's prescribed, and how to take it correctly. It's an optional layer of clarity — never a requirement to get your medication.

Atorvastatin 20mg

Why AR

Health literacy shouldn't require a screen-heavy device.

Cramming a full information display onto the device itself adds cost, clutter, and another thing to break. Instead, smartdose puts that depth in your phone's camera — available the moment you want it, invisible the moment you don't.

What AR does

Three moments where a scan says more than a label ever could.

Scan-to-learn at dispense

Every compartment carries a quick-reference overlay: what the medication is, why it's prescribed, and how to take it.

Interaction & food-timing overlays

Visual cues for medications that need food, spacing from other drugs, or specific administration steps.

AR-guided loading

During setup, the overlay confirms the right medication goes in the right compartment — reducing the loading errors reported across other devices in this category.

A deliberate constraint

AR is a layer, never a dependency.

One well-known "smart" health robot tried to be a dispenser, an assistant, and a companion all at once — and collapsed under the weight of its own feature list. smartdose's core promise works with zero AR, zero AI, and zero app open. Everything on this page is an enhancement on top of that, never a substitute for it.

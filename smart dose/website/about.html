About

A world where medication management just works.

We started smartdose after seeing how much of medication non-adherence isn't carelessness — it's a genuinely hard logistics problem that existing tools solve halfway.

Mission

Physically reliable. Emotionally trustworthy.

Our mission is to build the most physically reliable and emotionally trustworthy medication device on the market — one that treats hardware precision, caregiver peace of mind, and genuinely useful AI as equally non-negotiable, rather than picking one and treating the rest as marketing.

Vision

Independence, protected.

A world where no one — regardless of age, memory, dexterity, or how complicated their prescriptions are — misses a dose, takes the wrong pill, or loses independence because medication management is too hard to get right.

Where we started

The gap no one in this category has closed.

Nearly half of people on long-term medication don't take it exactly as prescribed. Traditional pillboxes demand organization that memory-impaired or overwhelmed patients don't always have. Reminder apps nag, but can't stop a wrong or duplicate dose. Automated dispensers solve dispensing but are frequently reported to jam, be hard to load, or bury their cancellation terms. And one ambitious "companion robot" tried to solve everything at once — and collapsed under its own feature list.

No product in the category combines physically fail-safe dispensing, real environmental safety monitoring, and AI that's actually predictive — in a device that looks like something you'd want on your kitchen counter, not next to a blood pressure cuff. That's the gap smartdose exists to close.

What we believe

The values behind every decision we make.

Capable, not clinical

Competent like a good nurse, not sterile like a hospital tray.

Warm, not saccharine

Caregiving is emotionally loaded. We stay steady and reassuring, never falsely cheerful.

Precise

"The right compartment rises" is our core credibility asset — quiet engineering confidence over hype.

Dignified

We never talk about patients as helpless. Independence and respect are constant undertones.

Transparent

Pricing, cancellation, and data practices, stated plainly and early — by design, not by accident.

Evidence over hype

We'd rather demonstrate reliability than claim it.

The team

Hardware engineers, clinicians, and caregivers, in the same room.

smartdose is built by a small team spanning mechanical and embedded engineering, mobile and AI development, and clinical/caregiving backgrounds — because a device this personal needed all three perspectives at the table from day one, not bolted on after launch.

Engineering priority №1

Jamming is the most common complaint in this category. We built against it first.

Independent reviews and caregiver communities consistently name the same failure across automated dispensers: hard-to-load trays and mechanisms that jam exactly when they're needed most. smartdose's compartment mechanism is designed and QA-tested against this specific failure mode from day one — not patched in after launch.

Design principles

Every decision traces back to one of these.

Look like a smart-home object

Not a medical device. The open lane in this category is premium consumer-electronics design applied to a health product.

The dispense moment is the hero

Every design decision should make taking medication feel simple, unambiguous, and calm.

Redundant, not reliant, feedback

Physical, audible, and digital cues each work independently — no single point of failure for knowing it's time.

Accessible by default

Large touch targets, high contrast, and simple language are the baseline — not a bolted-on setting.

Optional complexity

AR, AI, and advanced dashboards layer on top of a core experience that works with zero configuration beyond loading pills and setting a schedule.

Trust is designed, not claimed

Pricing, cancellation, data use, and reliability are demonstrated in the product — not just asserted in an FAQ.

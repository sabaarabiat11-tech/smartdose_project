How it works

Setup takes minutes. Trust takes zero effort after that.

smartdose is designed so a family member can walk a loved one through setup entirely by phone. Here's exactly what happens, from the box to your first automated dose.

01

Unbox and find a counter spot

smartdose is sized to sit anywhere a coffee maker would — no installation, no tools, no dedicated wiring.

02

Connect to Wi-Fi

The on-device display walks you through joining your home network in under two minutes.

03

Download the app and create an account

Available for iOS and Android. Add yourself as the patient, and invite one or more caregivers with their own login.

04

Build the medication schedule

Add each medication by name or by scanning the prescription label, then set dose times, quantities, and any special instructions.

05

Load the compartments, AR-guided

Point your phone camera at the device and follow the on-screen overlay confirming the right medication goes in the right compartment — the step most competitor devices leave you to guess at alone.

06

Let smartdose take over

At each scheduled time, the correct compartment rises and unseals automatically. A chime and an on-device light confirm it's ready — no phone required to know it's time.

For caregivers

What happens when a dose is missed.

Caregiver visibility isn't an afterthought bolted onto the patient experience — it's a parallel flow with its own logic.

  • A dose isn't taken within its scheduled window.
  • smartdose waits a configurable grace period, then flags the device display.
  • If still unresolved, every designated caregiver receives a push notification with the specific medication and time.
  • Repeated or irregular patterns are surfaced as a trend in the AI Analytics dashboard — before they become a crisis.

AI Analytics

From "did they take it" to "will they take it."

Most reminder tools log a checkbox after the fact. smartdose's AI is built to notice the pattern before the missed dose happens — and to explain it in a sentence a caregiver can actually act on.

Adaptive reminder timing

Learns when a specific person actually responds, rather than firing the same fixed alarm regardless of whether it works for them.

Predictive non-adherence detection

Flags emerging patterns — like a consistently late Tuesday evening dose — before they turn into a missed one.

Anomaly detection

Unusual dispensing activity — rapid repeated access attempts, irregular timing — triggers a caregiver review, not a false alarm.

Natural-language summaries

"Mom took 20 of 21 doses this week, missed Tuesday evening" — plain sentences instead of raw logs.

Personalized risk scoring

Combines adherence history and regimen complexity to prioritize which patients need attention first — most valuable at facility scale.

Opt-in population insights

De-identified, consent-first adherence insight — available to research and care partners only with explicit, transparent permission.

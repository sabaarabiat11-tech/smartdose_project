Why two plans

Patients and providers use smartdose very differently.

A patient needs a calm, single-schedule view of their own medication. A doctor or clinician needs to watch adherence risk across many patients at once and act on it fast. Rather than force both into one generic dashboard, the Patient and Doctor plans are built — and priced — around what each actually needs day to day.

Our cancellation commitment

Cancellation is a single tap inside the app — no phone call, no retention script, no fine print. This is a deliberate design choice: cancellation friction is the single most common complaint about products in this category, and we built directly against it.
